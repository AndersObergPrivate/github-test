# Q1 2024 - Developer Relations Roadmap

## 📅 Quarter Overview

**Time Period**: January 2024 - March 2024  
**Theme**: Foundation & Growth - Building Strong Developer Foundations

## 🎯 Key Objectives

1. Refresh and modernize developer documentation to improve developer experience
2. Launch community engagement programs to grow active developer base
3. Establish metrics and tracking systems for measuring DevRel impact
4. Build strategic partnerships with developer communities and influencers
